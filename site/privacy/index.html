Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

The Humspan app does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data.

It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking. It contains no code that connects to the internet. The only thing it keeps is your last session’s settings, and those stay on your device.

Who we are

Humspan is an ambient sound app for iPhone, iPad, Android and desktop, made by Adam Bailey (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Humspan app on every platform and this website. Questions go to adamjbailey7@gmail.com.

What the app collects

Nothing. We do not collect personal information, usage data, device identifiers, location, contacts, audio or anything else from the app. The sound is generated on your device as it plays; no recordings are made or uploaded, and the app never uses the microphone.

We have no servers that the app talks to, so there is nothing for us to store, analyse or hand over.

What stays on your device

So that Humspan opens the way you left it, it saves your last session’s settings in your device’s standard app preferences storage (UserDefaults on iOS and macOS, SharedPreferences on Android, and the equivalent on Windows and Linux):

SettingExample
Noise on or off, and its colorOn, brown
Session length1h 37m, or Until I stop
Binaural beat on or off, and its frequencyOff, 4 Hz
Boom on or off, and its gapOff, every 8 s
The level of each layer70%
Mono on or offOff

These settings are not personal information and never leave the device through Humspan. To delete them on Android, clear the app’s storage (Settings → Apps → Humspan → Storage → Clear storage). On iPhone and iPad, delete the app.

On Android, Humspan opts out of Google backup and device-to-device transfer, so its settings never leave the phone and a reinstall starts fresh. On iPhone and iPad, your own iCloud or device backup may include them, as it does for every app; that backup is handled by Apple under your account, and we never receive it.

Permissions

Humspan asks for no access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or files. It uses only what it needs to keep sound playing with the screen locked:

  • Background audio (iOS) and a media playback foreground service (Android), so a session continues while the screen is off.
  • Notifications (Android), only to show the playback controls in the notification shade and on the lock screen.
  • Wake lock (Android), declared by the media-controls component Humspan uses for those controls.

Humspan does not request internet access. The Privacy link beside Play opens this page in your browser; the app itself sends nothing.

Lock-screen and now-playing controls show the title “Humspan” and the layers that are on. That information is passed to your device’s operating system and goes nowhere else.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and shares no data with anyone.

You download Humspan from the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google run those stores under their own privacy policies and may give developers aggregated, anonymous statistics such as download counts. Those statistics do not identify you.

Security

Because the app collects and transmits nothing, there is no personal data of yours in transit or on any server of ours to protect. Your settings stay inside the app’s private storage, which your device’s operating system keeps separate from other apps.

If you email us, your message is held in our Gmail account, run by Google, protected by its account security, used only to reply to you, and deleted on request.

Children

Humspan collects no data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live).

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data a company holds about you. Because the app collects none, we hold nothing about you from it. If you email us, we use your message only to reply, and we will delete it on request.

This website

This website uses Google Analytics, run by Google, to count visits and see which pages are read. It sets cookies in your browser and sends Google the pages you view, the page that linked you here, your browser and device type, screen size, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. We use these reports only in aggregate, to understand how many people visit and how they find the site. We do not use them for advertising and do not combine them with any other data. Google handles this data under its privacy policy; you can see how it uses data from sites like this one at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

To opt out, block cookies for this site in your browser, use a content blocker, or install Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works the same without it. Its fonts are served from this site. The company that hosts it may also record standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to keep the site secure and working.

Analytics applies only to this website. The Humspan app does not include analytics or tracking and never will.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. If a change ever affected the app’s handling of data, we would also note it in the app’s release notes.
