Any exact length.

Drag the dial in 5-minute steps, tap a preset, or type an exact time such as 1h 37m 15s. Or choose Until I stop and it runs for days.

The clock is kept in played audio, not a timer the phone can throttle, so a session ends when it says it will, then fades out over its final minute.

Humspan opens with your last settings, stopped. It never plays on its own.

What it won’t do.

You set it once and put the phone down. After that, nothing should surprise you.

  • LoopThere is no file to repeat. The engine generates every sample in real time, for as long as the session runs.
  • Jump to the speakerIf headphones or Bluetooth disconnect, the session pauses. It resumes only when you press Play.
  • Stop abruptlyA timed session fades over its final minute and ends in silence. A call lowers the sound and the clock keeps time.
  • Ask for anythingNo account and no sign-in. Nothing you do in the app leaves your phone. Privacy
