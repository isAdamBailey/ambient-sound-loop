Common questions

How do I make it louder or quieter?
Use your device’s volume buttons. Humspan has no master volume; the level slider on each layer only sets the mix between layers.
Why did the sound pause when I unplugged my headphones?
On purpose. When headphones or Bluetooth disconnect, Humspan pauses instead of switching to the phone’s speaker. The screen shows “Headphones disconnected. Paused.” Press Play to carry on; the session keeps its remaining time.
Will it keep playing with the screen locked?
Yes. Lock the screen and put the phone down. Playback controls appear on the lock screen and, on Android, in the notification shade.
What happens when the timer ends?
A timed session fades out over its final minute and then stops in silence. Choose Until I stop on the dial if you want it to run until you press Stop.
Can I set a length that isn’t on the presets?
Yes. Drag the dial in 5-minute steps, or tap Exact to type hours, minutes and seconds.
What happens during a phone call?
The sound is lowered for the call and comes back afterwards. The session clock keeps running.
I can’t hear the binaural beat.
A binaural beat needs a different tone in each ear, so use headphones. With Mono on, the two tones mix in both ears and you hear the beat as a gentle pulse in the sound instead.
Does it repeat or loop?
No. Every sample is generated as it plays, so there is no recording and no loop point, however long the session runs.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. Humspan works fully offline and never connects to the internet.
Why did it open stopped?
Humspan always opens with your last settings, stopped, so it never starts making sound on its own. Press Play when you’re ready.
How do I reset it?
On Android, clear the app’s storage (Settings → Apps → Humspan → Storage → Clear storage), or delete the app and install it again. On iPhone and iPad, delete the app and install it again. Humspan then opens as on first launch.
