## Description

Humspan plays colored noise, a binaural beat and a slow boom for any length you choose, from a few minutes to days. Every sample is generated as it plays, so there is no recording and no loop point.

THREE LAYERS
• Noise: white, pink or brown. White is flat, pink is softer at the top, brown is deep and round. Change color mid-session without a seam.
• Binaural beat: two tones, one in each ear, 2 to 10 Hz apart. Labelled by frequency, nothing more. Use headphones for this layer.
• Boom: a low, resonant strike that alternates left and right every 8, 4 or 2 seconds.

Turn on any mix. Each layer has its own level, and every change is smoothed, so nothing clicks. A Mono switch plays the same sound in both ears, for listening with one earbud.

ANY EXACT LENGTH
Drag the dial in 5-minute steps, tap a preset (30m, 1h, 2h, 4h, 8h), or type an exact time such as 1h 37m 15s. Or choose Until I stop. The clock counts played audio, so a session ends when it says it will. A timed session fades out over its final minute and ends in true silence.

SET IT ONCE
• Play fades the sound in over 5 seconds.
• Lock-screen and notification controls let you stop without unlocking.
• If headphones or Bluetooth disconnect, the session pauses. Sound never moves to the speaker.
• A call lowers the sound, and the clock keeps time.
• Humspan opens with your last settings, stopped. It never plays on its own.

NO ACCOUNT, NO ADS
No sign-in, no ads and no analytics. Nothing you do in the app leaves your phone.
