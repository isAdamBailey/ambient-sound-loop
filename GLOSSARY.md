# Ambient Sound Loop

The shared language for a procedural listening session, shown the same way on every platform.

## Language

**Session**:
A stretch of rendered audio from the moment it starts until it finishes or is stopped.
_Avoid_: Playback, track, loop

**Session screen**:
The single screen of the app. It has the same layout, controls, and behaviour on desktop, iOS, and Android.
_Avoid_: View, page, home

**Platform**:
A place the app runs: desktop, iOS, or Android.
_Avoid_: Driver, backend, target

**Until I stop**:
A session with no set duration. Its clock shows elapsed time.
_Avoid_: Open-ended, infinite

**Last session**:
The noise color, volume, session length, and binaural beat settings last used on that device. Restored stopped, never resumed.
_Avoid_: Preset, saved mix
