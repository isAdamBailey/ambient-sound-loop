# Surface brief: marketing site (site/)

Scope: site/index.html (home, Persuade), site/privacy/ and site/support/ (Read). Static, dependency-free, self-hosted fonts, nothing fetched from third parties.

Audience: someone choosing a sleep/ambient sound app on their phone, comparing against apps that loop a recording. Job: believe Humspan never loops and runs for any exact length, then go to their store. Action: App Store / Google Play links (not live yet; shown as "coming soon" until URLs exist). Proof: product facts only (procedural engine, exact length, fade to true silence, headphone pause). No testimonials, numbers, or sleep/health claims (PRODUCT.md). Privacy and support pages exist for store submission: App Store needs a privacy policy URL and a support URL; Play needs a privacy policy URL. Contact is the owner's Gmail. The app collects nothing; the site may add analytics later, so the policy covers the site separately.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A night drawn as one span. The page refuses the sleep-app hero (moon, navy gradient, phone mockup over a starfield) and shows instead what a session is: a measured stretch of time that starts softly, never seams, and ends in silence.

OWN-WORLD: The Night Tide Table, unchanged: ground #05090D, parchment ink, faded ink, hairline rules, sea blue only on measured amounts (the session band, the dial arc), no amber at rest. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for words, its Mono with tabular figures for every time and frequency. Flat; depth is tone and line.

STORY: The visitor reads one line that names the offer, sees the night span with its times, understands the sound is made as it plays and fits any length, reads the three layers as table rows, sees what it refuses to do (loop, jump to speaker, stop abruptly), and goes to the store.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Wordmark top left with Privacy/Support right. Headline at left ("Sound for exactly as long as you need it"), short line under, store buttons. Below, full content width, the night span: an hour-ticked hairline axis from 10:42 PM to 6:30 AM in Mono, a sea-blue band ramping in over the first minutes and out over the last minute, annotations pinned above/below like tide entries, the length 7h 37m 00s in large thin Mono. Store buttons are the primary action, above the fold on desktop and phone.

FORM: Overnight timeline, position 3 of 6 on the ordered list, seed:c9fd14289446b275. Signature interaction: the band draws once from left to right on load (reduced motion: static), and hovering/focusing an annotation highlights its time on the axis. Motion grammar: one draw, ease-out, nothing else moves.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
